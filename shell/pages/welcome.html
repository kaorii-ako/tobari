Welcome to Tobari

A quieter Chromium. Let's set the few things worth choosing — it takes under a minute, and everything can be changed later.

  • Ads and trackers stop before they load.Built in, nothing to install.
  • Nothing is sent home.The only background traffic is the weekly filter-list update.
  • Your Chrome extensions work.Install them from the Chrome Web Store, as usual.
