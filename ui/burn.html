Burn this session

Forget everything websites and Tobari remember about your browsing, then start fresh.

Goes

  • Open tabs
  • History
  • Cookies and logins to sites
  • Cache and site storage
  • Site permissions

Stays

  • Bookmarks and reading list
  • Settings and blocking choices
  • Assistant setup
  • Downloaded files

Press and hold, or hold Enter. Tobari restarts when it's done.
