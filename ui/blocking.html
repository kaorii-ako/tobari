Blocking and privacy

0 requests blocked since Tobari started.

Filter lists

Requests to ad and tracker hosts are cancelled before they leave the browser. Lists update once a week; nothing else is sent.

ListRules usedUpdated

Sites with blocking off

None. Use the shield next to the address to turn blocking off for one site.

    Scam and malware warnings

    Warns before you open a site Google lists as phishing or malware. The check runs on this device, but keeping the list fresh contacts Google about every 30 minutes, even when you aren't browsing. Off by default.

    Always on

    HTTPS-Only
    Pages load over https. If a site has no https, Tobari asks before using http.
    Cookies
    Third-party cookies are blocked.
    WebRTC
    Calls go through a proxy or not at all, so your local and public IP stay private.
    Device access
    Camera, microphone, location, notifications, USB, Bluetooth, serial and MIDI requests are refused.
    Telemetry
    None. Tobari sends no usage data, crash reports or pings. While idle it contacts nothing but the filter-list hosts above, once a week.
